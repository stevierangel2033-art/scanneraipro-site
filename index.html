Your iPhone is already a scanner.
This makes it a good one.

Scanner AI Pro turns paper into clean, sharp PDFs in a couple of seconds — then lets you actually edit them. Sign, redact, retype the text that's printed on the page, and export to whatever format you need.

Coming soon to the App Store.

Scans that don't look scanned

A wide-angle camera view means you don't have to hold your phone up by your forehead to fit a sheet of A4. Live edge detection finds the page, auto-capture waits until the whole document is in frame and your hand is steady, then shoots at full sensor resolution and flattens the perspective.

A PDF editor, not a text box

Open any document and work directly on the page. Add text, drop in an image or logo, place a signature, or hide a block of sensitive information. Pinch to zoom in on small print. Tap a line of recognized text and retype it in place — the original is covered with the page's own background colour so the edit looks like it was always there.

Sign it and send it

Draw your signature with a finger, drag it exactly where it belongs, resize it, and it's baked into the PDF. No printing, no scanning back in.

Export to anything

PDF, JPG, plain text, Word, Excel, or PowerPoint. Text-based formats are built from the text recognized in your scan, so you get something you can actually edit — not a picture of a document.

Search everything

Every scan is read with on-device text recognition, so you can find a document by what's written inside it, not just what you named it. Organize with folders, star what matters.

Private by design

Scans, text recognition, and documents all stay on your device. We don't run servers that hold your paperwork, because we don't want to. Read the policy.
